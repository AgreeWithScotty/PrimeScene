0.2.38
- Apply one HTTPS-only TLS policy across sign-in, authorization, catalog, artwork, media and update requests.
- Require certificate/hostname verification and TLS 1.2 or newer; do not inherit environment proxies or write TLS debug traffic keys.
- Remove the non-Windows credential TCP listener and use an owner-only private filesystem socket with mutual authentication.
- Refuse credential operations when private IPC is unavailable, without a TCP fallback.
- Discard invalid or unprotectable legacy plaintext credentials; block use and report cleanup failure if deletion is refused.
- Reject linked, replaced, foreign-owned and non-regular private files, and restrict saved session headers to public browser preferences.
- Normalize credential field-name variants in logs and preserve safe diagnostic failures and TLS certificate-verification details.
- Keep the current presentation, navigation, genre artwork, pagination and provider playback choices. Restart Kodi after updating the credential service.
